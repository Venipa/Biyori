---
title: Torrents
description: Follow RSS and search feeds for new episode releases in Biyori.
---

**Settings -> Torrents** configures RSS feeds, search feeds, sort order, download directory, and the app used to open a torrent.

The **Torrents** page lists items from those feeds. Filters live in the same settings panel.

Biyori does not bundle a BitTorrent client. It finds releases and hands files or magnets to the app you choose.
